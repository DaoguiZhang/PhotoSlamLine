feat(mapper): introduce error-guided sampling and aggressive line-aware pruning

1. Error-Guided Keyframe Sampling (Hard Example Mining):
   - Replaced uniform random sampling in `useOneRandomSlidingWindowKeyframe` with Roulette Wheel Selection based on L1 loss (`tracking_error_`).
   - The optimizer now prioritizes training on keyframes with lower PSNR, effectively reducing local artifacts.

2. Aggressive Pruning Mechanism:
   - Upgraded `densifyAndPruneWithLineAwareness` to tackle the numerical black-hole caused by over-seeded line Gaussians.
   - Introduced anisotropy pruning and isolation checks to forcefully remove deformed, spike-like artifacts that mask photometric errors.

3. Tensor Dimension & Sync Fixes:
   - Fixed fatal shape mismatch bugs for `rotation_` during `increasePcd`, `densifyAndClone`, and `Split` (ensuring strictly `[N, 4]`).
   - Maintained global sync for shadow tensors like `xyz_init_` across all point addition/removal operations.

4. Performance Tweaks:
   - Moved `Ll1.item<float>()` fetch after `loss.backward()` and `cuda::synchronize()` to prevent implicit Device-to-Host sync from blocking the computational graph pipeline.
