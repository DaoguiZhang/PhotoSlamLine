Snapshot completeness audit (existing r0 checkpoints)

All existing frame_500/1000/1500/end snapshot.pt files contain 16 tensors:
  0 xyz, 1 features_dc, 2 features_rest, 3 scaling, 4 opacity, 5 rotation,
  6 is_line, 7 line_dir_w, 8 xyz_init, 9 max_radii2D,
  10 xyz_gradient_accum, 11 denom, 12 exist_since_iter, 13 point_ids,
  14 ref_kf_id, 15 coord_event_id

They do NOT contain:
  - Adam exp_avg / exp_avg_sq / step
  - optimizer param-group learning rates (AdamOptions)
  - the live optimizer object or its state keyed by TensorImpl
  - per-keyframe remaining_times_of_use / pyramid usage
  - a full live iteration schedule

meta.txt contains iteration, optimizer_steps, kf count, Gaussian/line counts,
active/default SH and the render size.  cameras.csv contains the native training
camera poses, image paths and intrinsics.  masks.pt/mask_cameras.txt contain the
undistort masks.  These are sufficient for offline rendering/PSNR, but NOT for
a faithful optimizer resume.

Therefore the existing end snapshot cannot be restored as a complete optimizer
state.  Rebuilding Adam from a PLY/snapshot would be a silent state reconstruction
and was not done.

The controlled follow-up used a live natural-end capture instead:
evidence/checkpoint_followup_r1/undertrain_r1/terminal_state.txt

That live state has:
  - 6 Adam groups
  - exp_avg=1 and exp_avg_sq=1 for every group
  - LRs: 5.03314e-05, 0.0025, 0.000125, 0.05, 0.005, 0.001
  - CPU and CUDA generator states
  - 168 keyframes, 450378 Gaussians, 2080 line Gaussians
  - frozen_opt_steps=8712, Adam step=8625

The Adam step (8625) is 87 lower than frozen_opt_steps (8712); those optimizer
steps were not identical counters in this build (some iterations returned
without a view/update).  Do not claim a one-to-one optimizer-step identity.

Important timing note: the r0 "end" checkpoint (C optimizer_steps=8337,
174/171 KFs) was captured at input frame 2000 during final refinement, not at
the process's final terminal state.  The live r1 terminal state is later
(8712 steps), so its absolute PSNR is not the same checkpoint as r0/end.
